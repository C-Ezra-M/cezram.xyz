The results of my finals, which I had in May, just came in!

![Results of my finals](/blog/finals-results/results.png)

I get it, you may not speak Polish. But here, I'll translate the stuff you need to know.

- Polish: I got a 78% score on the written exam, and a 100% score on the oral exam. I did not take an advanced-level exam in this subject.
- Math: I got a 98% score on the basic-level exam, but only 36% on the advanced-level exam. Poland has abandoned oral math exams.
- English: The written exams went well. I had a 92% score on the basic-level exam, and 90% on the advanced-level exam. I also got a 100% score on the oral exam.
