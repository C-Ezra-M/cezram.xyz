As of August 30, I have signed the European Citizens' Initiative ["Stop Killing The Internet: No Digital ID & No Age Verification"](https://eci.ec.europa.eu/066/public/).

I am one of the first 2,500 signatories to the initiative, and among Poland's first 150 signatories.

![Confirmation of signing the European Citizens' Initiative "Stop Killing The Internet: No Digital ID & No Age Verification", with the signature identifier obscured.](/blog/skti-support/skti-initiative-signed.png)

## What is Stop Killing the Internet?

**Stop Killing the Internet** was created by [Gamer's Voice](https://gamersvoice.org/), the UK chapter of Stop Killing Games, to raise awareness of government and corporate influence over the Internet. This includes controversial procedures, like ID verification and censorship.

To this end, I made a badge that you can put up on your website. I previously [shared it on Reddit](https://www.reddit.com/r/StopKillingGames/comments/1v1mivy/web_badge_for_stop_killing_the_internet/) on July 20, and I'll do so here as well.
