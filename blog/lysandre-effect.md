The rise and fall of modern billionaires looks like the rise and fall of Lysandre, the owner of Lysandre Labs in *Pokémon X and Y*. To put his iniquity into perspective, let's compare it with real-world billionaires. I am not bringing up names of any real-world individuals, since it is a generalized view.

## The Case of Lysandre

Lysandre is portrayed as a philanthropist and the owner of Lysandre Labs, who created the Holo Caster, a device that allows people to communicate with each other through holograms. He sought a more beautiful world, implying he didn't want what's considered "negative" or "ugly", like strife, struggle, war, or despair.

To this end, he made a lot of donations to better the world. One of the people he helped is Corbeau, who would later idolize Lysandre for his generosity. As time went on, Lysandre realized his donations weren't doing as much as he hoped, and he implied that the expanding human population was to blame, citing their inability to share limited resources.

Lysandre is also the boss of Team Flare, initially created for the youth to come together and help each other. This team wasn't always evil, but as Lysandre became more of a misanthrope, Team Flare's goals changed as well. The team sought to make money and eliminate everyone who doesn't meet their standards, in the name of a better world.

Team Flare also set up a machine to record Holo Caster conversations. It was discovered that it was used for espionage, but later, Sina and Dexio, assistants of Professor Sycamore, destroyed it.

## How Does it Compare to the Real World?



## The Usefulness Trap

Lysandre wanted a world without what's considered "ugly", like strife, struggle, war, or despair. He genuinely wanted to better humanity. He made donations. His laboratory built the Holo Caster.

Some billionaires created or aided the creation of tools that humanity found useful, like the Google search engine, the Windows operating system, or the Facebook social network.

Later, for selfish reasons, they make the experience worse for everyone. Advertisements, tracking, or both became the norm in those tools. This is enshittification, and some companies are already in the third stage of their lifecycle, which is when they have already pissed off their business customers and started taking everything for themselves.

## The Real-World Analogies

Lysandre's age is unstated, implying that having such an evil mindset is not connected to one's age.

The subject of regional government is not really touched upon in Pokémon. However, we do know that Lysandre is descended from royalty; his ancestor is AZ's younger brother. As such, he is one of the few who know where the ultimate weapon is, as AZ, the former king of Kalos, is the person who buried it in the first place.
