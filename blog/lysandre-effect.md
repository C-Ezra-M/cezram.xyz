The rise and fall of modern billionaires looks like the rise and fall of Lysandre, the owner of Lysandre Labs in *Pokémon X and Y*. To put his iniquity into perspective, let's compare it with real-world billionaires. I am not bringing up names of any real-world individuals, since it is a generalized view.

## The Case of Lysandre

Lysandre is portrayed as a philanthropist and the owner of Lysandre Labs, who created the Holo Caster, a device that allows people to communicate with each other through holograms. He sought a more beautiful world, implying he didn't want what's considered "negative" or "ugly", like strife, struggle, war, or despair. Sounds like a noble cause, but ends up being a publicity stunt.

To this end, he made a lot of donations to better the world. One of the people he helped is Corbeau, who would later idolize Lysandre for his generosity. As time went on, Lysandre realized his donations weren't doing as much as he hoped, and he implied that the expanding human population was to blame, citing their inability to share limited resources.

Lysandre is also the boss of Team Flare, initially created for the youth to come together and help each other. This team wasn't always evil, but as Lysandre became more of a misanthrope, Team Flare's goals changed as well. The team sought to make money and eliminate everyone who doesn't meet their standards, in the name of a "better" world.

Team Flare also set up a machine to record Holo Caster conversations. It was discovered that it was used for espionage, but later, Sina and Dexio, sent by Professor Sycamore, destroyed it.

Sycamore himself used to be friends with Lysandre, therefore he was disappointed when he found out that Lysandre is the boss of Team Flare. This can also explain why he resigned as director of the Pokémon Research Lab after the Team Flare incident.

## How Does it Compare to the Real World?

Lysandre's age is unstated, implying that having such an evil mindset is not connected to one's age. Indeed, billionaires of all ages are as evil as each other. Doesn't matter if you're just above 40. Doesn't matter if you're over 80.

The subject of government is not really touched upon in Pokémon, at least not for the time the games take place at. However, we do know that Lysandre is descended from royalty, specifically from AZ's younger brother. AZ is the former king of Kalos and the one who built and buried the ultimate weapon, so Lysandre knows where it is. Many real-world governments keep such dark secrets, many of which have been passed down for centuries.
