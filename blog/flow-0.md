## Overview

This camp runs on points, just like BFRK, BFRP, or BFRP NEO. At the end of each challenge, I will average the scores of each team. The contestant with the lowest score on the lowest-scoring team is eliminated.

I will be giving out Fluxer Plutonium for winning. This is my first camp with real prizes. They will be given out after the finale.

The winner is guaranteed to get 1 year of Plutonium. The ones placing 2nd and 3rd get 1 month of Plutonium. How much more Plutonium I give out depends on how many contestants decide to sign up.

### Activity

- If you skip three challenges in a row (either by excusing or not doing them), you are eliminated. The same happens if you don’t do two challenges in a row without excusing.
- If you do two challenges in a row while you have any strikes, one of your strikes is removed.
- If you excuse, you will get the average score of everyone who did the challenge.

## Tokens

- All tokens can be used **in B episodes only**. This is to make the game a bit more unpredictable.
- **Advantage Token (AT)**: Gives you an advantage in the challenge, which will only count towards your score after calculating the team averages.
- **Protect Token (PT)**: Protects you from being eliminated. Used automatically.
- **Thief Token (TT)**: Choose another contestant, and then choose 1:
  - Steal 3% of their total score, rounded up.
  - Steal 20% of their points gained from the challenge, rounded up.
- **Exchange Token (XT)**: Choose another contestant, and your score from the challenge is swapped with theirs. Only works if both you and the target do the challenge.

> 🆕 **Update (June 23, 2026)**
>
> If multiple contestants use a Thief Token on the same contestant, the total amount of points stolen is distributed equally between their users, and the target doesn't lose more points.
