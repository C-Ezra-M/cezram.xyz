What? I'm not going to use the mechanical keyboard anymore? Turns out, I am not very used to mechanical keyboards, but first I have to assess if this is because I've been using it around the same time as when my standing desk is broken, as it doesn't rise anymore. The motor is broken and the manufacturer doesn't sell individual components.

The seller says that only the matrix and the charger work, but I believe the keyboard works too. The palmrest looked flawless from the photos.

Dad told me that replacing the keyboard would be sufficient, though I do know it is riveted into the palmrest instead of simply being screwed in. Though I still looked up videos on how to replace the keyboard on that model, and in fact it **is possible** without having to replace the palmrest's top shell.
