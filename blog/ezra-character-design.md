---
title: Explaining Ezra's Character Design
---

It's nearly one year since I drew Ezra's current character design. It has gone through multiple minor changes since then, but most elements have remained the same.

## 2023(?): The Original Design

According to the dates recorded in the filesystem, this design is dated May 2024, but I remember doing much of the drawing on my phone while on an international flight, which couldn't happen in May. Maybe around summer 2023? I don't know for sure!

![](/blog/ezra-character-design/c-ezra-m-design-2024.png)

Ezra appears to be portrayed as a tall young adult. He has brown eyes, a light complexion, and brown hair, except for head hair, which is dyed turquoise. He wears a hat with a Quick Ball design, a green-and-orange hoodie with a green hood, an orange kangaroo pouch, brown cuffs, light gray drawstrings with silvery ends, and the hexagonal logo in front, washed-out blue pants, white socks with a light blue wavy line around them, and white sneakers with black and red patterns and red laces.

The hoodie's bicolored design is itself inspired by [Sendr](https://sendrbrand.com/) hoodies, which I heard of on TikTok, which I no longer use. Note, I do not own *any* Sendr hoodies. Never have.

![Andrew Balzer, owner of the Sendr brand, in a dark blue-and-red hoodie](/blog/ezra-character-design/sendr-with-hoodie-1.jpg)

## Late 2024: The Kiddy Redesign

I started this in early December 2024, and then completed it just before Christmas.

![](/blog/ezra-character-design/c-ezra-m-pfp-youngster-with-bg.png)

The design above the waist is largely unchanged. However, Ezra overall has a shorter stature now. The hoodie has simpler cuffs and a tag resembling the Polish flag, with a triangle wave symbol, derived from the first letters of my first and last name.

The design under the waist has gone through more changes than that. Ezra now wears dark blue shorts with a geometric pattern made of red lines, white socks, and black-and-red shoes with white soles.

The pose is based on the one of the Youngster Trainer type, specifically from a sprite in the 1997 Spaceworld demo. It was not available to see during normal gameplay, but was found in the leaked ROM.

![Youngster sprite, original size](/blog/ezra-character-design/youngster-sprite-sw97-1x.png)

![Youngster sprite, scaled up by 10x](/blog/ezra-character-design/youngster-sprite-sw97-10x.png)

My character in *Pokémon Sword* wore the same shorts as Ezra does, which come from the uniform worn by Gym Trainers in Hammerlocke Stadium, themselves inspired by Lance's outfit from *Pokémon HeartGold and SoulSilver*. You can get the uniform itself after defeating Raihan.

I started the drawing for my new profile picture on October 17, 2026. Then, I completed it the next day. Right now, this design only features the head.
