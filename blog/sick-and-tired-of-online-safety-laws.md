I am sick and tired of those "protect the children" battle cries as an excuse to invade our privacy.

Here in Poland, I am very worried about [Chat Control](https://fightchatcontrol.eu/), an EU-wide piece of legislation that is going to mandate indiscriminate scanning of messages and ID verification on online platforms. AI is not panacea, and mass surveillance isn't either. Please call your MEPs to reject Chat Control, because this is just mass surveillance and censorship.

Over in America, please call your senators to reject the [KIDS Act](https://www.govtrack.us/congress/bills/119/hr7757). It's just the same thing. Do it! For the sake of all of us!
