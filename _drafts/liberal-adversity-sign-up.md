## After signing up

If you get accepted, I will send you a welcome message and ask you for a timely response. Once I receive the response, I will send you your **Secret ID**. It is a code consisting of three letters and three digits. In no circumstances may you reveal your Secret ID to anyone, or use it in your challenge entry.

An example Secret ID is `XYZ 267`. All IDs that start with `XYZ` are placeholders and will never be assigned.

I'm aiming for 40 contestants. To ensure we get many contestants, [spread the word!](#first-token-raffle) If you get rejected, you won't hear from me.

## How to play

You will have two options to submit challenge entries and cast votes.

- Forms (Secret ID required) - use a form linked in the episode.
- Contact via Fluxer, Matrix, Stoat, or Discord (Secret ID not required, except when switching accounts or platforms)

## General schedule of the game

After the first challenge as teams, contestants will be eliminated based on who has the lowest *overall* score, from the team that *gained* the fewest points in the challenge.

During the camp, you can earn tokens. There are only two kinds of tokens.

- ![Advantage Token](/advantage-token.png) **Advantage Token** - using it will give you an advantage in the challenge.
- ![Protection Token](/protection-token.png) **Protection Token** - if you're about to be eliminated because of points, this token is consumed, and you're not eliminated. The next person with the fewest points that doesn't have a Protection Token will be the one eliminated.

### First token raffle

There is a field in the form that requires you to say how you were referred to this camp. When you put in one name, you give the person mentioned a chance to win a token.

The jackpot contains one Advantage Token and one Protection Token.
